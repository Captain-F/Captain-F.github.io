## Research interests {#research}

My research focuses on multimodal machine learning and knowledge graphs for digital humanities, particularly the documentation and analysis of intangible cultural heritage. I also work on natural language processing and sentiment analysis.

## Selected publications {#publication}

- **Intangible cultural heritage image classification with multimodal attention and hierarchical fusion.** Fan, T., Wang, H., & Deng, S. *Expert Systems with Applications*, 2023.
- **Research of Chinese intangible cultural heritage knowledge graph construction and attribute value extraction with graph attention network.** Fan, T. & Wang, H. *Information Processing & Management*, 2022. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ipm.2021.102753)
